A script page that knows what you are about to type.

Six screenplay elements, one keyboard. Pines formats each line as you write, so you never stop to drag a margin.

Enter next line

Finish a line and Pines moves to the element that usually follows: character after action, dialogue after character.

Tab accept suggestion

Character names and locations you have already used appear as you type. Press Tab to fill them in.

Alt + 1 to 6 switch type

Jump between scene, action, character, dialogue, parenthetical and transition without leaving the keys.

Dual dialogue

Two speakers talking at once sit side by side, the way they do on a real shooting script.

Tamil typing

Type Tamil phonetically with a built-in keyboard mode, scene by scene or across the whole script.

Focus and fullscreen

Dim everything but the line you are on, or go fullscreen. Find and replace (Ctrl+F) and a scene navigator are one key away.

Every draft kept. Every colour in order.

Save a version before a big rewrite and restore it as a new draft if you change your mind. Mark revisions with the colours film crews already know: white, blue, pink, yellow, green and goldenrod.

When the script is done, the shoot is half planned.

Pines reads your draft and builds scene, character, location and production reports on its own. Group scenes into shooting days, set call times, print call sheets and keep a manual budget alongside.

Proof that your story was yours first.

Create a dated certificate with a SHA-256 fingerprint of the exact script. Change one character and the fingerprint changes, so you can verify later that a draft is untouched.
